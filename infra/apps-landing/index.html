doax apps

Lightweight hosting for doax-apps — static apps that hold only resource references and read everything through the registry's Fixed Interface: resolve, verify the package digest, invoke. Deploying is a registry operation, governed by your namespace and recorded in the audit log.

Deploy

$ doax login --email you@example.com
$ doax app deploy @yourns ./dist
# → https://yourns.app.doax.io/

Owner or publisher role in @yourns required — the same rule as publishing a resource. Static files only; no server runs on your behalf.
